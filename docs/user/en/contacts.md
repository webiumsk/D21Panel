# Contacts

Contacts are the customers (and suppliers) you put on your documents. Adding a contact once means you can reuse it on every future invoice.

## Add a contact

Open **Contacts** in your company and choose **New contact**. Fill in the details you need:

- Name and address
- Tax identifiers (registration number, tax ID, VAT number)
- Email (used when you send a document by email)
- Default payment terms and bank details, if relevant

## Import contacts

Have an existing list? Use the **import** option to bring contacts in from a spreadsheet (CSV/XLSX). Map the columns once and Satflux creates or updates the contacts.

## Peppol / e-invoicing IDs

If you send e-invoices (for example Slovak e-invoicing), your customers may need a Peppol participant ID. Satflux can derive it from the tax ID where possible, and the e-invoicing readiness checklist flags any contacts still missing one.
