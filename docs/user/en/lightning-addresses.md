# Lightning addresses

A **Lightning address** looks like an email - `you@your-domain` - and lets anyone pay you over Lightning without asking for a fresh invoice each time.

## Requirements

Your store needs a working **Lightning** connection first. Once Lightning is ready, Lightning addresses become available.

## Create an address

1. Open your store and go to **Lightning addresses**.
2. Choose a username; the domain is provided for you.
3. Save. The address is immediately payable.

You can create more than one address (subject to your plan) - for example one per product or campaign.

## Where to use it

Put it on your website, social profiles, or a tip jar. Wallets that support Lightning addresses can pay it directly. It also pairs well with Points of Sale and Pay Buttons.
