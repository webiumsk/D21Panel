# Export for your accountant

When it is time to do the books, Satflux can package your documents for your accountant in the formats they use.

## Accountant package

From your company, open **Accountant export** and choose the period. Satflux assembles a package that can include:

- Issued documents (invoices, credit notes) and their PDFs
- Recorded expenses
- Structured data your accountant's software can import (for example ISDOC, and Pohoda XML where applicable)

Because the package always carries a structured file plus the human-readable PDFs, your accountant can import what their tool supports and read the rest.

## Sharing directly

If you would rather your accountant work in the app, you can instead [share the company with them](/documentation/share-with-accountant) - they get live access without exporting anything.
