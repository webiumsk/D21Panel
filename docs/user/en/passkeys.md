# Passkeys & device unlock

A **passkey** lets you unlock Satflux on a device with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN - so you do not have to type your 24-word recovery phrase each time.

## How it fits with your recovery phrase

Your **recovery phrase is still the master key** to your account. A passkey is a convenient way to unlock on a device you already trust; it does not replace the phrase and cannot recover your account on its own.

## Add a passkey

- Right after you register, Satflux offers to add a passkey - the quickest moment to do it.
- Any time later, open your **account** settings and add one there.

You can add passkeys on several devices and give each a label.

## Remove a passkey

Remove a passkey from your account settings - for example if you lose a device. Removing a passkey does not affect your recovery phrase or your data.

## If a device has no passkey

On a new device, sign in with your recovery phrase, then add a passkey there for next time.
