# Point of Sale

A **Point of Sale** (PoS) turns your store into a simple checkout for in-person payments - a market stall, a café, or a pop-up shop.

## Create a Point of Sale

1. In your store, open **Apps** and create a **Point of Sale**.
2. Add your products (name, price) or use a keypad for ad-hoc amounts.
3. Open the PoS link on the device you will use at the counter.

## Taking a payment

Pick items or type an amount, show the customer the QR code, and they pay over Lightning or on-chain. The screen confirms when the payment settles.

## Tips

- Bookmark the PoS link or add it to your device home screen for quick access.
- Use a dedicated device or browser profile at the counter.
- Pair it with a [Lightning address](/documentation/lightning-addresses) for tips.
