# Store Lightning addresses

A **store Lightning address** is a reusable address hosted on your own store's domain - `username@your-store-domain` - that anyone can pay over Lightning without you having to create and share a new invoice each time. Put it on your website, socials, or a tip jar.

> This is different from [connecting a wallet by its Lightning address](/documentation/connect-with-any-lightning-address). Here you are **hosting your own** receive addresses on your store; there you point the store's backend at an external wallet.

## Requirements

Your store needs a working Lightning setup first (see [Wallet connection overview](/documentation/wallet-connection-overview)). Once Lightning works, hosted addresses resolve on your store's domain.

## Add an address

1. Open your store and go to **Lightning addresses**.
2. Click **Add address** and choose a **username**; the domain is fixed to your store's Lightning domain.
3. Optionally set **Advanced settings**: currency code, minimum and maximum amounts (in sats), and invoice metadata.
4. Save. The address is immediately payable. Use the copy and QR actions to share it.

You can create several addresses (for example one per product or campaign), up to your plan's limit. A counter shows how many you have used.

## Plan limits

The number of hosted addresses depends on your plan - the Free plan includes a small number and Pro raises it (see [Plans & limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits)). When you reach the limit, Satflux prompts you to upgrade. The exact number is shown on the page and on the Pricing page.
