# Connect your first wallet

Before you can accept payments, your store needs a wallet. Satflux walks you to the **wallet connection** screen right after you create your account.

## What you can connect

- **Lightning** - connect your Lightning node or a supported Lightning backend to accept instant, low-fee Lightning payments.
- **Lightning address** - once Lightning works, you get a reusable `you@your-domain` address people can pay any time.
- **On-chain** - your BTCPay store can also accept regular on-chain Bitcoin payments.

The wallet you connect belongs to **your** BTCPay store - Satflux never custodies your funds.

## Steps

1. Go to your store and open **Wallet connection**.
2. Choose how you want to accept payments and follow the prompts to link your wallet.
3. When the wallet is ready, the store is unlocked and you can create Points of Sale, Pay Buttons, and Lightning addresses.
