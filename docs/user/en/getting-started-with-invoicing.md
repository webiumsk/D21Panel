# Getting started with invoicing

Satflux invoicing lets you issue proper invoices, quotes, proformas and credit notes, keep contacts and stock, match bank payments, and hand a clean package to your accountant.

Invoicing is **local-first**: your invoicing data is stored privately in your browser and end-to-end encrypted, then synced across your devices. Because of that, remember to keep your recovery phrase backed up.

> Invoicing is available on the **Pro** plan (and Enterprise). See [plans and limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits).

## The first-run wizard

The first time you open **Invoicing** with no company yet, a short setup wizard appears and hands you off to create your first company. After that, a **Getting started** checklist on your invoice list guides you through the rest:

1. Complete your company profile (address and tax IDs).
2. Add your logo.
3. Add your first customer.
4. Issue your first invoice.

The checklist disappears once every step is done, and you can dismiss it any time.
