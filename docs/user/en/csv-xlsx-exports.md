# CSV / XLSX exports

Beyond the accountant package, Satflux lets you export records as spreadsheets so you can analyze them yourself or feed them into other tools.

## What you can export

- **Invoices and other documents** from a company
- **Contacts** and **expenses**
- Store-level data such as invoice/payment history

## How it works

Open the relevant list, apply any filters you want (date range, status, type), and choose **Export**. Larger exports are prepared in the background and offered as a download when ready.

Files are generated as **CSV** or **XLSX**. Exports of sensitive data are audit-logged.
