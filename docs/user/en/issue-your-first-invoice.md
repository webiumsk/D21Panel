# Issue your first invoice

With a company and at least one contact in place, you are ready to invoice.

## Create the invoice

1. In your company, open **Invoices** and choose **New invoice**.
2. Pick the **customer** (or create one on the spot).
3. Add **line items** - description, quantity, unit price and VAT rate. Stock items and previously used lines are suggested as you type.
4. Set dates (issue, due, delivery) and any notes.

While it is a **draft** you can edit freely. When you **issue** it, Satflux allocates the official document number from your series and locks the record for audit purposes.

## Get paid

On the invoice you can enable payment options:

- **Bank transfer** - the invoice shows your bank details and, where supported, a payment QR code.
- **Bitcoin / Lightning** - link the invoice to your store so the customer can pay on-chain or over Lightning.

## Send it

Use **Send by email** to deliver the PDF to your customer, or download the PDF to share it yourself. You can also export ISDOC/UBL where supported.

## Track payment

Mark invoices paid manually, or let [bank payment matching](/documentation/bank-payment-matching) reconcile them automatically from your bank statements.
