# Share a company with your accountant

You can give your accountant (or a colleague) access to a company without handing over your recovery phrase. Sharing is **end-to-end encrypted** - only the people you invite can read the data.

## Invite someone

1. Open your company's **profile** and find the **Sharing** card.
2. Create an **invite** for the person you want to add.
3. Send them the invite link. When they accept, they join the company and can work in it under the role you gave them.

An invited accountant does not need their own paid plan to work in a company you own.

## Managing members

From the same card you can see active members and **revoke** access. Revoking takes effect immediately - the removed member can no longer open the company.

## Rotating the key

If you remove someone and want to be certain they cannot read anything written afterwards, use **Rotate key**. Remaining members are re-invited under the new key. Data they had already downloaded before removal stays with them - that is true of any shared document.
